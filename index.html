FAQ

Common questions.

What is the platform?
ORBITAL is a frontend prototype for a future aerospace investment platform. It demonstrates how users will be able to explore space technology opportunities in a clean, professional interface.
How does the opportunity work?
The final structure is yet to be defined. Placeholder data is shown for demonstration. No real financial instrument is being offered at this stage. All figures are illustrative only.
How will wallet connection work?
In the future, you will be able to connect a supported cryptocurrency wallet to verify eligibility and participate. The button currently opens a placeholder modal with no real connection logic.
Which wallets will be supported?
We plan to support MetaMask, WalletConnect, and Coinbase Wallet initially. Exact availability and implementation timeline will be announced at a later date.
When will the platform launch?
This is a frontend prototype. Backend development, wallet integration, and regulatory compliance are under consideration. No launch date has been set.
Is this financial advice?
No. Nothing on this website constitutes financial, investment, or legal advice. All data is placeholder and for demonstration purposes only. Always consult a qualified professional.
